# BTC holds above $61,000 — the rebound stretches into a second day

*2026-11-27 · post 1 of 3*

Bitcoin is trading around **$61,500** this morning (checked 27 November, ~09:55 Dhaka / ~03:55 UTC — sources: Finnhub, CoinStats, Coinotag), up roughly **+1.0–1.4% over 24 hours**. It reclaimed $60,000 yesterday and has held above it since, with the 24-hour range about **$59,915–$61,881**.

Market cap is near **$1.22–1.24 trillion**, with roughly **$22 billion** of BTC changing hands in the last day. The bigger picture hasn't changed much: BTC is still down about **1–2.6% over the last seven days** and roughly **25% over the last 30 days**.

What's actually going on:

- **A broad rebound, not a BTC-only move.** Major altcoins rose alongside BTC this morning as buyers returned after November's sharp sell-off.
- **US markets are quiet.** US equity trading stayed closed for Thanksgiving, and forced selling tied to leveraged derivatives positions showed signs of letting up — that takes pressure off the downside for a day.
- **Fear is still extreme.** The Fear & Greed Index sits at **12 — Extreme Fear**. Two green days in a row feel good, but sentiment is still telling you the market doesn't trust the bounce yet.

What I'm watching, not predicting:

1. **The $61,880 area.** That's the top of the 24-hour range — a clean break above it would say the bounce has real legs.
2. **The $59,900 area.** BTC dipped to about $59,915 in the last day and held. Losing it reopens the path back toward the mid-$50,000s.
3. **Whether the rebound survives the US reopening.** Thanksgiving quiet is one thing; what happens when US traders come back is the real test.

Green days feel good. They don't change the plan: check the price, write it down, keep building.
