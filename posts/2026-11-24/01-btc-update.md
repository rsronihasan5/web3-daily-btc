# BTC drifts around $61,000 — a flat day inside a fearful month

*2026-11-24 · post 1 of 3*

Bitcoin is trading around **$61,000** this morning (checked 24 November, ~09:51 Dhaka / ~03:51 UTC — sources: Finnhub, Blockdeck, Coinotag), roughly **flat over 24 hours**. Different sources read anywhere from about −1.5% to +0.6% depending on the exchange and the hour — that spread is the story of the day: nobody has control right now.

The 24-hour range is wide, about **$60,358–$64,446** — a $4,000 swing with no direction. Market cap is near **$1.23 trillion**, with roughly **$38.7 billion** of BTC changing hands in the last day.

What's actually going on:

- **The bounce has stalled.** After two green days and holding $60,000, BTC is chopping sideways instead of pushing higher. The $64,446 high got sold, the $60,358 low got bought — a market waiting for a reason.
- **Fear is still extreme.** The Fear & Greed Index sits at **11 — Extreme Fear**. November is still down about **21%** from the 31 October close of $77,582, and BTC is roughly **52% below its all-time high of $126,080**.
- **Leverage keeps getting flushed.** About **$96.8 million** of BTC positions were liquidated on Hyperliquid in the last 24 hours. Open interest there fell 3% while price barely moved — the move is spot-driven, not leverage-driven.

What I'm watching, not predicting:

1. **The $60,000 line.** It held twice this week. A clean break below it reopens the weekend low near $55,130.
2. **The $64,446 high.** Clearing it would be the first sign the bounce has legs instead of just a pause.
3. **Whether fear stays extreme while price holds.** Extreme fear plus a holding price is usually where the market either finds a floor or finds a trap — the next few days will tell which.

Flat days after a crash are boring to watch and important to record. I check the price, write it down, and get back to building.
